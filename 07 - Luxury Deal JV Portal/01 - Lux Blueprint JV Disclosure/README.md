LUX BLUEPRINT LLC

JV DISCLOSURE

*Deal Splitting, Partnering, and Joint Venture Arrangements*

## 1. NATURE OF THE COMPANY

Lux Blueprint LLC (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") is an education company engaged in providing real estate investing education, coaching, tools, and related resources. The Company is NOT a real estate investment company and does not engage in the purchase, sale, assignment, or rental of any real property. The Company does not participate in, facilitate, broker, or otherwise engage in real estate transactions of any kind.

## 2. DEAL SPLITTING AND PARTNERING

No customer, student, client, or user of the Company, including but not limited to buyers or users of any of its educational products, coaching programs, membership communities, educational events, business tools, or related offerings, will ever partner with the Company on any real estate transaction in any capacity. The Company does not enter into joint ventures, deal splits, co-wholesaling arrangements, or any other form of real estate transaction partnership with its students, customers, or clients.

## 3. SEPARATE ENTITY DISCLOSURE

Any reference made to "partnering," "deal splitting," "JV arrangements," "joint ventures," or similar terminology involving Tyson Smith, Matthew Tooker, or any member of their team will be conducted exclusively through Lux Off Market LLC, a separate and distinct legal entity from the Company. Tyson Smith and Matthew Tooker are owners and/or principals of both Lux Blueprint LLC and Lux Off Market LLC. However, these are separate legal entities with separate operations, and the activities of one do not constitute the activities of the other.

## 4. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Any deal, property, or business referral made by a student, customer, or client to entities outside of the Company, including but not limited to Lux Off Market LLC, will in no way affect the Company, including but not limited to financial gains, financial losses, legal issues, disputes, claims, or liabilities arising from business conducted as an effect of such referrals. The Company, Tyson Smith (in his capacity as co-owner of the Company), and Matthew Tooker (in his capacity as co-owner of the Company) shall not be held liable for any losses, damages, disputes, claims, or liabilities arising from any real estate transaction conducted between a student, customer, or client and Lux Off Market LLC or any other third-party entity.

By submitting any deal, property, or transaction to Lux Off Market LLC or any affiliated entity for joint venture or deal-splitting purposes, you agree to indemnify and hold harmless Lux Blueprint LLC, its owners, officers, employees, agents, and affiliates, including Tyson Smith and Matthew Tooker in their capacity as owners or representatives of the Company, from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, expenses, and liabilities (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising out of or related to such transaction.

## 5. NO RIGHTS, RESPONSIBILITIES, OR BENEFITS THROUGH THE COMPANY

No rights, responsibilities, obligations, or benefits referred to in sales materials, membership websites, webinars, coaching calls, educational materials, or any other Company content in regards to real estate transactions with Tyson Smith, Matthew Tooker, or their team are in any way connected to the Company. Any such rights, responsibilities, obligations, or benefits pertain solely to Lux Off Market LLC or other entities operated independently of the Company. Details regarding which entity will facilitate any deal split, joint venture, or partnership arrangement will be provided within the relevant materials furnished to the student, customer, or client.

## 6. ASSUMPTION OF RISK

The Company stands to gain no benefit or loss from any real estate transaction of its students, customers, or clients. Any benefit, obligation, risk, or liability arising from a real estate transaction will pertain exclusively to entities outside of the Company. You acknowledge that real estate investing involves inherent risks, including the potential loss of capital, and that you are solely responsible for your own investment decisions and due diligence.

## 7. NO PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

Any opinions, guidance, feedback, or commentary given by any member of the Company’s staff, coaches, or representatives regarding a specific real estate investment, transaction, or deal are the personal opinions of that individual and do not represent the opinion or position of the Company. Such opinions are not to be construed as legal, financial, tax, or professional advice. You should consult a licensed attorney, CPA, or other qualified professional for advice regarding any specific real estate investment transaction.

## 8. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By purchasing any product or service from the Company, enrolling in any coaching program, or participating in any educational offering of the Company, you confirm that you understand and agree to the following:

(a) The Company is an education company only and will in no way partner with you on any form of real estate transaction.

(b) Any deal-splitting or joint venture arrangement will be conducted exclusively through Lux Off Market LLC, a separate legal entity.

(c) The Company will not be liable in any way for your real estate dealings, losses, or disputes.

(d) You agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Company, its owners, and its affiliates from any claims arising from real estate transactions you participate in.

(e) You have been advised to seek independent legal and professional counsel before engaging in any real estate transaction.
