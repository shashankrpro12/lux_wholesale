## Lesson text

[Submit potential deals HERE](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeoHSn1Sup1fddCE5KnTG6nnjPxi6MLFt1rD_aWCrzR3TXWEQ/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=107072279417213903853)

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

By submitting a deal through the JV form, [you're agreeing to our Lux Blueprint JV Disclosure](https://www.skool.com/lux-blueprint-academy-9786/classroom/576c5cbc?md=1e76ed7e21cc42cc8aa33c98802349ac&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=5640a423877f48ba81b0a18bd5dfec58) and you're agreeing to JV with Lux Blueprint if you get the contract. This means:

- If you lock the contract, we co-wholesale the deal together
- The assignment fee or profit gets split per the structure below
- You're committing to work with us on this deal from contract to close

This isn't a soft inquiry. Submitting the form = you're in.

🔵 FIRST

Submit your lead through the JV form after you've had a real conversation with the seller and confirmed interest in selling.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

You must have already spoken to the seller and gone through the full discovery conversation.

To submit a deal, ALL of these must be true:

✓ Property must be located within one of our [Approved Buy Boxes](https://www.skool.com/lux-blueprint-academy-9786/classroom/576c5cbc?md=072d1f4d1a414c7aab36791c4475c4b9&utm_campaign=skool_link_classroom&utm_content=5640a423877f48ba81b0a18bd5dfec58)

✓ You must have talked to the seller and gone through the script

✓ Seller gave you an asking price, price range, or pricing expectation

✓ Seller confirmed they would consider an actual offer on the property

✓ Property must be off-market and direct to seller

✓ Seller has a valid reason to sell (not...make me an offer, for the right price, etc)

If the answer is no, keep working the lead first.

REQUIRED INFORMATION

Your submission must include:

• Your contact info (name, email and phone number)

• Seller full name (not their LLC, trust, etc)

• Seller phone number

• Property address (street details, city, state, and postal code)

• Asking price or pricing expectation

• Lead source

• Seller motivation and situation

• Seller timeline

• Notes from your conversation

We need real information so our team can properly underwrite the deal and step in if needed.

If we don't know:

• who the seller is

• why they're selling

• what price they're thinking

• or what was discussed...

...we cannot effectively help move the deal toward a contract.

Incomplete submissions will not be reviewed.

ON-MARKET DEALS WILL BE DISQUALIFIED IMMEDIATELY.

🔵 SECOND

We underwrite it and text you your max offer.

You'll get a message like this:

"Hey [Your Name], your MAO for [Property Address] is $XXX,XXX. Reply STOP to unsubscribe."

That text is your max offer. We're not providing coaching or support through the form submission process.

🔵 THIRD

UNDERWRITING TIMELINE

Our underwriting team reviews deals in the order they're received.

We typically will have a response to you within 48 hours during business days.

Deals submitted Saturday or Sunday: We will do our best to get them out by Tuesday, but some may be delivered Wednesday depending on volume.

🔵 FOURTH

You call the seller and go get the contract.

Most deals happen fast. You've got 5 business days to work it. Call, follow up, and lock it up.

This is your deal. You brought it. You work it. You win.

IF THINGS STALL

After 5 days, if you haven't been able to lock it up, we step in to help push it across the finish line.

We're not here to take deals from you. We're here to make sure they don't die.

SPLIT STRUCTURE

If YOU lock the contract:

- Profit split 50/50

If WE step in and close it:

- Profit split 25 percent to you, 75 percent to us

BOTTOM LINE

Bring solid leads. Move fast. Work it hard for 5 days.

If you get stuck, we step in and help you get it done so you still get paid.

The only way you lose is if you don't submit the deal.
