#### Guarantee Terms

If Client has not completed a Closed Deal within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date (the "Guarantee Period"), Client is eligible for a refund of the Program Fee amounts actually paid by Client as of the date of the refund request, provided Client has met all requirements outlined in Section 4.2 below and is not disqualified under Section 4.4. A "Closed Deal" means any wholesale real estate transaction (including an assignment of contract or a double close) in which Client participated and for which closing occurred during the Guarantee Period. A transaction under contract but not yet closed at the end of the Guarantee Period counts as a Closed Deal if it closes within thirty (30) days after the end of the Guarantee Period, and Company may defer its refund decision until then. This Section 4 provides a conditional refund only; it is not a guarantee of any result.

#### Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a refund under this Section 4, Client must: (a) Make and document a minimum of fifty (50) Qualifying Offers during the Guarantee Period. A "Qualifying Offer" is a good-faith written offer (including by email, text message, or a purchase agreement presented to the seller) made directly to the owner of record of a distinct property, or the owner's authorized representative, at a price calculated using the methods taught in the Program. Multiple offers on the same property count as one Qualifying Offer; (b) Submit each Qualifying Offer through the offer-tracking form designated by Company within seven (7) days of making the offer; (c) Include the following information for each Qualifying Offer: date the offer was made; property address; offer amount presented to seller; a copy or screenshot of the written offer; seller’s reaction/response; and next steps taken or planned; (d) Be current on all payments due under Section 3 throughout the Guarantee Period and at the time of the refund request; and (e) Complete all training modules in the Program classroom during the Guarantee Period and remain in compliance with this Agreement throughout the Guarantee Period.

#### Refund Process

Client must submit a refund request in writing to [info@luxblueprint.com](mailto:info@luxblueprint.com) within seven (7) days following the end of the Guarantee Period. Company will review submitted offer documentation within fourteen (14) business days after receiving the request and may request reasonable additional verification. Company will issue approved refunds to the original payment method within thirty (30) days of approval. Upon issuance of a refund, (a) any remaining unpaid installments of the Program Fee are cancelled, (b) Client's access to the Program and the license in Section 6 terminate, and (c) the provisions that survive this Agreement remain in effect.

#### Disqualification

Client will be disqualified from receiving a refund if Client: fails to submit the required fifty (50) documented Qualifying Offers; provides false or misleading information in Client's offer documentation; requests a refund outside the specified timeframe; is not current on all payments due under Section 3; initiates a chargeback or payment dispute in violation of this Agreement; shares Program access or Program Materials in violation of Section 5 or Section 6; has been suspended or terminated.
